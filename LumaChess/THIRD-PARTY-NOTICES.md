## Stockfish.js / Stockfish 19

LumaChess can optionally load the Stockfish 19 Lite single-threaded WebAssembly chess engine from `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/stockfish@19.0.1/bin/`. The engine is provided by the Stockfish.js project, is licensed under GNU General Public License version 3 (GPL-3.0), and is not bundled in this source ZIP; the browser fetches it at runtime.

Project and license information: https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/
Package information: https://www.npmjs.com/package/stockfish

Stockfish is a chess engine, not a general-purpose conversational AI. It evaluates chess positions and selects moves locally in the browser after its WASM files have been fetched.
